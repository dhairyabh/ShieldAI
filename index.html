THE PROCESS

How ShieldAI Works

Three autonomous steps — intercept, analyze, neutralize. All in under 200ms.

01

Intercept

AI middleware hooks into incoming calls and messages via Twilio SIP routing and WhatsApp Business API — transparent to the user.

02

Analyze

Groq-powered LLM parses content for scam tactics: forced urgency, fake UPI requests, deepfake voice patterns, and identity spoofing.

03

Neutralize

Returns a strict JSON verdict: NEUTRALIZE, MONITOR, or ESCALATE. Zero hallucinations. Zero false executions.
